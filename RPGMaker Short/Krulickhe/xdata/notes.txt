Humans

Humans are a well established species on Earth, and the first intelligent and sentient species of Earth as well.  They have been around for far longer than zarakites and any other intelligent species of Earth-origin, and in modern times are sometimes referred to as "ancients" or "archaics" in modern English language, as well as "human."  Modern pure-blooded humans, which make up the largest population group of present-day people of Earth, when taking into account the entire world population of people, are also known as "common humans."  None of these terms are derogatory, and are used in newspapers, professional documents, and more.  It has been this way for many generations now, including going far back before \P[1] and any other human alive today was born.
